## Overview
Designed and developed a lightweight AI-assisted Air Traffic Control (ATC) simulation concept. This project focuses on enhancing aviation training scenarios by integrating with Microsoft Flight Simulator (MSFS) and providing an immersive VR experience using Meta Quest 3 headsets. It bridges the gap between traditional simulation and next-generation AI-driven interactions.

## Responsibilities
- Conceptualize and design the AI-assisted ATC simulation architecture.
- Integrate the AI simulation with Microsoft Flight Simulator.
- Adapt the simulation concept for immersive virtual reality training environments.
- Combine AI, simulation, and XR technologies to create a realistic and effective training tool.

## Results
- Successfully extended a training concept into an immersive VR environment.
- Demonstrated the viability of combining lightweight AI solutions with established simulation platforms for aviation training.
